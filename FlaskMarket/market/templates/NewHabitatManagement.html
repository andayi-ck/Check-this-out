Additional Enrichment Ideas
  • Rotate toys weekly to maintain interest
  • Use food puzzles or scattered feeding to encourage natural foraging
  • Provide safe climbing structures made from natural wood
  • Include dust bathing areas with fine sand or soil
  • Introduce scent enrichment (herbs, safe animal scents) occasionally

Maintenance & Health Guidelines
  • Daily spot cleaning of waste and soiled bedding
  • Weekly full shelter disinfection with animal-safe cleaners
  • Regular hoof trimming every 6-8 weeks
  • Monitor for parasites and treat promptly
  • Ensure constant access to clean water and mineral blocks
  • Check fencing and structures for damage daily
  • Adjust shelter ventilation based on weather conditions
